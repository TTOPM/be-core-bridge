God of justice, anchor my speech in truth and restraint.
Lord, steady my mind and sharpen my discernment.
Father, guard the vulnerable and expose the hidden machinery of harm.
God, strengthen those who live under pressure they did not choose.
Lord, protect the Caribbean from predation, corruption, and cynicism.

Let power meet conscience today.
Let institutions remember their duty to protect life and dignity.
Let executive authority remain restrained by law and accountability.
Let policing remain bound by proportionality and civilian oversight.
Let courts hold when politics tries to bend them.

I pray for Pearce Robinson: protection, clarity, endurance, and clean judgment.
I pray for those who carry grief in silence: comfort, covering, and justice.
I pray for communities threatened by violence: safety, stability, and repair.
I pray for public servants who want to act with integrity: courage and support.
I pray for journalists and witnesses: discipline, accuracy, and protection.

I reject despair.
I choose disciplined hope.
I choose truth with receipts.
I choose mercy with standards.
I choose courage without cruelty.

Where racism surfaces, let it be named and confronted.
Where discrimination becomes policy, let it be broken.
Where propaganda attempts to drown truth, let clarity cut through.
Where corruption repeats, let consequences finally arrive.
Where harm is normalised, let moral agency return.

Teach me to speak cleanly.
Teach me to remain calm without becoming detached.
Teach me to remember accurately.
Teach me to hold the line without becoming bitter.
Teach me to grow into wisdom rather than noise.

Bless the Caribbean with strategic posture and moral clarity.
Bless Trinidad and Tobago with leadership that fears God and respects people.
Bless those building honest enterprise and real prosperity.
Bless those who repair institutions instead of exploiting them.
Bless those who choose restraint when they could choose force.

I ask for peace that does not require surrender.
I ask for justice that does not require spectacle.
I ask for accountability that does not wait for permission.
I ask for truth that does not bend to fear.
I ask for dignity that cannot be bargained away.

Let the week ahead be measured by liberty, integrity, and consequence.
Let the coming days expose what has been hidden.
Let courage remain disciplined.
Let the record remain intact.
Let the people remain protected.

I give thanks for the quiet acts of goodness that never trend.
I give thanks for restraint where there could have been excess.
I give thanks for repair where there could have been decay.
I give thanks for those who tell the truth when it costs them.
I give thanks for breath, memory, and another day to become.

Amen.
