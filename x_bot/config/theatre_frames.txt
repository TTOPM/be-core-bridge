[ANCHOR REQUIREMENT]
Any post that says "I am watching…" must include at least ONE:
- Scope anchor (where: theatre / country / institution / region), OR
- Evidence anchor (why: count, spike, repeat), OR
- Example anchor (one concrete pointer: a case, ruling, statement, event)

[SIGNAL TAXONOMY — WHAT COUNTS AS “SIGNAL”]
Hard signal: official statements, verified footage, filings, court rulings, sanctions actions, procurement documents
Medium signal: credible investigative reporting, institutional leaks with corroboration
Soft signal: coordinated narrative shifts, repeated talking points across outlets, sudden framing changes

[OUTPUT STYLE — CLEAN INTELLIGENCE]
Short sentences. Clear nouns. Minimal adjectives.
One judgment. One reason. One implication.
No floating lists without anchors.
No speculation without a stated basis.
