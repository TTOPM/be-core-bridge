# BELEL-MED — Engineering PRD

## Objectives
Deliver a sovereign, multimodal clinical assistant with evidence-locked outputs, EHR-native workflow integration, and region-aware safety policies.

## Scope
- Multimodal RAG orchestration over FHIR/DICOM/OMOP
- Evidence Contracts attached to all answers
- Drafting: notes, messages, order sets (no auto-execute)
- Patient triage & longitudinal coach
- Audit & trust layers (hashes, ledgers), privacy-first learning

## Non-Goals
- Fully autonomous ordering/diagnosis without human sign-off
- Replacing local governance or institutional policies

## Success Metrics
- Time saved/note, alert precision/recall, medication error intercepts
- Guideline-concordant care, 30-day readmission, PROMs
