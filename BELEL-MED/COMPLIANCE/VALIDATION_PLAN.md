# Clinical Validation Plan

## Phases
1) Shadow mode (no impact): compare model outputs vs. clinician ground truth.
2) Prospective, low-risk domains with DSMB oversight.
3) Phased expansion with pre-specified endpoints (safety, efficacy, workflow).

## Metrics
- Alert precision/recall, time saved/note
- Med error intercepts, guideline adherence
- Clinical outcomes: readmission, ED bounce, PROMs
