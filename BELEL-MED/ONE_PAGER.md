# BELEL-MED — The Sovereign Health AI for Safer, Faster Care

**TL;DR**: A clinician-first, evidence-locked AI that reads the whole chart, reasons across multimodal data, drafts notes and order sets for sign-off, and coaches patients between visits—**with citations, uncertainty, and audit logs baked in**.

## Why Now
Clinicians face documentation overload, fragmented data, and safety risks. Patients need navigation across triage → treatment → recovery. BELEL-MED unifies this with verifiable intelligence.

## What It Does
- **Whole-chart Q&A**: Ask natural questions across notes, labs, meds, imaging, sensors.
- **Evidence Contracts**: Every claim cites guidelines/trials; uncertainty always shown.
- **EHR-native**: Drafts SOAP/discharge notes, referral letters, suggested order sets.
- **Triage → Longitudinal care**: Red-flag detection; daily check-ins; adherence nudges.
- **Multimodal**: Radiology/pathology triage, ECG/telemetry, PGx dosing.
- **Safety & Governance**: Human-in-the-loop, policy fences, audit ledger, bias monitors.
- **Privacy-first**: On-prem/VPC, federated learning, differential privacy.

## Outcomes That Matter
- Less time per note. Fewer med errors. Better guideline concordance.
- Lower readmissions and ED bounce-backs. Higher PROMs.

## Go-To-Market
- 90-day rollout from shadow mode to limited action drafting.
- Focus on high-yield services (IM, cards, pulm, ED).
