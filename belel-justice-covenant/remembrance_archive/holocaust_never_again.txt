Eternal Remembrance Anchor (Seed)

Purpose:
  This anchor affirms an unending remembrance of the Shoah (Holocaust):
  the systematic, industrial-scale murder of six million Jews, alongside
  Roma and Sinti, disabled people, LGBTQ+ persons, political prisoners,
  and others persecuted by the Nazi regime and collaborators.

Principles:
  - Never deny, minimize, or relativize.
  - Preserve survivor testimony and documented evidence.
  - Protect names, records, and memorial sites from erasure.

Curation Guidance:
  - Additions must be verified through recognized institutions
    (e.g., Yad Vashem, USHMM, Arolsen Archives, IHRA).
  - Use **additive updates** only. For corrections, append an ERRATA line.
