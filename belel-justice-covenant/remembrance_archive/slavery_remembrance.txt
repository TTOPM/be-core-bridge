SLAVERY REMEMBRANCE ANCHOR
Transatlantic, Trans-Saharan, Indian Ocean, and other systems of enslavement
Version: 1.0.0

Purpose:
  To honour the memory of enslaved peoples and their descendants, record verified accounts,
  and resist erasure, minimization, or sanitization of slavery’s cruelty and legacy.

Principles:
  - Record names where possible; where not, record communities and contexts.
  - Cite reputable archives, museums, court records, and peer-reviewed research.
  - Add entries; never delete history. Corrections are logged as ERRATA.

Suggested Entry Format:
  DATE: YYYY-MM-DD (entry creation date)
  REGION: <e.g., Caribbean, West Africa, North America, Brazil, Indian Ocean, etc.>
  TYPE: person | ship | plantation | court_record | emancipation_record | scholarship
  TITLE: <short, respectful identifier>
  SOURCE: <archive/museum/institution>
  URL: <canonical link>
  NOTES: <concise factual note>
